Quick estimate · ~5 min

Skip the transactions. Fill in the Travel, Home, and Food tabs from memory — miles driven and flown, electricity and gas, diet. Captures the biggest physical drivers; your goods & services stay uncounted.

Complete picture · ~20 min

Upload a CSV of ~12 months of transactions (a Category column helps). Review the merchant rules until the ● rows — the top 80% of spending in each category — look right, then fill in Travel, Home, and Food.

Electricity

Advanced: upload your hourly usage

Many utilities export interval data (PG&E "Green Button" / Share My Data, etc.). Upload the CSV — columns with a timestamp and kWh (15-minute rows are summed to hours) — and your footprint uses your hours × your grid's hourly intensity instead of the typical-household assumption. The file stays in this browser. Or try the Portland example (an electric-heated home on a hydro grid — winter-peaked usage that misses the grid's dirty late-summer months).

Sources — electricity: EIA-930 2024 hourly generation by fuel for your balancing authority, calibrated to EPA eGRID 2023 annual rates, weighted by OpenEI TMY3 residential hourly load shapes, plus IPCC AR5 upstream fuel-cycle emissions and eGRID delivery losses. Physical grid average — voluntary green-power plans don't change it (the wires deliver the shared mix). Gas/oil/construction factors measured from the CoolClimate Network household-calculator API. Water & sewage is deliberately omitted: physically it is ~4.25 kWh per 1,000 gallons (EPA/EPRI) ≈ 25–110 kg CO₂e/yr for typical households — a rounding error (CoolClimate's much larger water figure is expenditure-based and double-counts water heating).
