Your carbon footprint, from what you actually did.

Most footprint calculators ask your household size and your ZIP and hand you a regional average wearing your name. This one is built from your own spending, your own miles, your own utility bills and your own diet — and it shows you every assumption it used to get there.

Start with your transactions Quick estimate instead Free, no account, nothing to install. The complete route takes about 20 minutes; the quick one about 5.

Nothing here is a black box. The methodology page lists every constant the model uses and where it came from, including the ones that make the answer worse for the model — where it's known to run high, where it's known to run low, and why. If you think a number is wrong, it is a link away from being checkable.

What leaves your device

Your transactions stay in your browser. The CSV is parsed locally and the results live in this browser's storage — there is no account, and no server ever sees the file.
  • Merchant names and statement categories are sent to a shared classification cache, and for merchants nobody has classified yet, to Anthropic's API. Amounts, dates and account names are never sent.
  • Usage analytics are first-party and cookieless: no third-party script, no persistent identifier, nothing you typed. That's why there's no cookie banner.
  • Clearing this browser's data deletes everything, because that is the only copy.
